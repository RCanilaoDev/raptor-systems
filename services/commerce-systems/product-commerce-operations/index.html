Commerce Operations // Operating Role

Growth creates catalog work. Catalog work needs control.

Every new product, collection, price, promotion, image, description, tracking change, app, and template update can affect search visibility, customer experience, analytics, and storefront accuracy. Operations keeps those changes coordinated.

01 // DATA

Is the product information complete and consistent?

Titles, descriptions, pricing, variants, images, metadata, product type, collections, and supporting content should be accurate before the item reaches customers.

02 // MERCHANDISING

Does the catalog make products easy to discover?

Collections, related products, navigation, internal search, featured items, and promotional placement need to reflect current inventory and business priorities.

03 // RELEASE

Can changes move without breaking something else?

Launch dates, theme changes, product updates, discount logic, tracking, and marketing destinations need QA before and after publication.

04 // MEASUREMENT

Can the team see what happened after the change?

Analytics, search performance, conversion events, product behavior, and operational notes help separate a successful release from one that simply went live.
