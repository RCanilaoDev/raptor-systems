01 DISCOVERY

Your services need to be found.

Missing search information, weak internal links, and technical issues can make important pages harder to discover. Issues that may limit discovery:

  • Missing page titles
  • Generic meta descriptions
  • Unclear service headings
  • Missing service pages
  • Thin service content
  • Weak internal links
  • Broken internal links
  • Blocked search crawlers
  • Unintended indexing restrictions
  • Missing XML sitemaps
02 CONTENT

Your pages need to explain the value.

Thin or unclear content can leave visitors guessing about what you offer, who you help, and why they should get in touch. Issues that may obscure value:

  • Unclear service descriptions
  • Vague customer benefits
  • Undefined target audience
  • Missing service details
  • Generic website copy
  • Unexplained industry jargon
  • Weak business differentiation
  • Missing customer testimonials
  • Unanswered customer questions
  • Unclear next steps
03 EXPERIENCE

The next step needs to be obvious.

Mobile friction, confusing page layouts, and buried contact options can interrupt the path from interest to inquiry. Issues that may interrupt inquiries:

  • Buried contact information
  • Unclear calls to action
  • Confusing page layouts
  • Hard-to-find inquiry forms
  • Lengthy contact forms
  • Difficult mobile navigation
  • Small tap targets
  • Broken contact links
  • Distracting pop-ups
  • Unclear submission feedback
04 EVIDENCE

Improvements need to be checked.

Search performance and visitor behavior help identify what is working and where the website needs more attention. Areas that may need attention:

  • Search visibility trends
  • Search query relevance
  • Search click-through rates
  • Service page traffic
  • Visitor engagement patterns
  • Mobile visitor behavior
  • Contact link clicks
  • Inquiry form completions
  • Lead tracking accuracy
  • Conversion path drop-offs
