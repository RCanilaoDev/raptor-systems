The Opportunity // Your Existing Website

A good-looking website can still leave people unsure what to do next.

01DISCOVERY

Your services need to be found.

Missing search information, weak internal links, and technical issues can make important pages harder to discover.

02CONTENT

Your pages need to explain the value.

Thin or unclear content can leave visitors guessing about what you offer, who you help, and why they should get in touch.

03EXPERIENCE

The next step needs to be obvious.

Mobile friction, confusing page layouts, and buried contact options can interrupt the path from interest to inquiry.

04EVIDENCE

Improvements need to be checked.

Search performance and visitor behavior help identify what is working and where the website needs more attention.
