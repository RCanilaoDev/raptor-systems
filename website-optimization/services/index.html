Potential Scope // Defined After Consultation

The right work depends on the website.

01WEBSITE

Fix the issues that get in the way.

Review and improve agreed technical, mobile, navigation, and usability issues. Prioritize fixes by their relevance to your business and the effort required.

02SEARCH

Strengthen the signals on your pages.

Depending on the findings, improve page titles, headings, metadata, internal linking, indexing conditions, or relevant structured data.

03SERVICE CONTENT

Give visitors useful answers.

Improve agreed service pages with clear descriptions, relevant questions, service-area information where appropriate, and credible reasons to contact your business.

04CONVERSION PATH

Connect the page to the next step.

Align service-page content, calls to action, and contact options. Make the route to a call or inquiry easier to understand and use.

Example // A Local Service Business

A service page should do more than name the service.

For an auto repair business, a brake service page might explain common symptoms, available work, what to expect, and how to request an appointment. Relevant internal links and a clear contact action connect that information to the next step.

This is an example of the approach. Your business, website, and agreed priorities determine the actual work.

Questions // Before We Start

Know what you are agreeing to.

Do I need a new website?

The starting point is improving your existing website. If the review identifies a larger rebuild or platform limitation, we discuss it before adding work to the scope.

Does every client receive the same services?

No. Your consultation and website review determine the priorities. The proposal defines the pages and improvements included in your engagement.

Can you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. Search visibility and inquiries depend on several factors. We commit to the agreed work, verification, and clear reporting on the evidence available.

How do we get started?

Request a website consultation. Share your website, the services you want to promote, and what you want to improve. We discuss fit, scope, timing, and fees directly.
