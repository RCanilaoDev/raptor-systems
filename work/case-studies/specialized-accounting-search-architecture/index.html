Service Structure, Structured Data & Local Alignment

A complex accounting-services website needed clearer service architecture, machine-readable business context, and better alignment between the website and local search signals.

Disclosure

This work predates Raptor Consulting Group and is presented as prior professional operating experience. Client identity is withheld.

CASE // 01Search Systems
SITUATIONComplex service model → clearer search system
PROOF TYPEClient-Reported Outcome
STATUSANONYMIZED
CASE RULENo claim beyond the evidence.
01 // Situation

The operating problem.

The business offered services that were difficult to present cleanly through a conventional website structure. The site needed to explain the service model to prospective customers while also giving search systems clearer context about what the business did.

Local profile work was handled in coordination with another function, so the website and profile signals also needed to stay aligned rather than drifting into different descriptions of the business.

02 // Work Performed

What the operator actually did.

The case is described in terms of implementation and operating responsibility, not generic capabilities.

01Business Model Review

Translate the actual service offering into a clearer website structure before adding technical markup.

02Structured Data

Implement machine-readable context that reflected the visible business and service information rather than inventing new claims.

03Website / Profile Alignment

Keep the website's service and business signals aligned with the local profile work handled by the broader team.

04Post-Change Review

Review the live implementation and watch for whether the revised structure supported clearer search and customer behavior.

03 // Result

What changed.

CLIENT-REPORTED OUTCOME

Inbound calls became more consistent after the restructuring and alignment work.

No public conversion percentage or ranking lift is claimed because those figures were not preserved as verified case-study metrics. The result is presented at the level supported by the original client feedback.

What supports the result statement.

05 // Raptor Relevance

Why this experience matters to the current system.

Raptor's current methodology takes the operating lessons from this work and formalizes them: inspect the full system, verify what is real, identify dependencies, document the change, and validate what happened after implementation.
