Evidence Standard // How to Read the Cases

The label matters as much as the result.

A delivered system, a directly observed result, a client-reported outcome, and prior operating experience do not carry the same proof. The case record identifies the evidence class so the reader can interpret the claim correctly.

01System Delivered

A website, architecture, migration, technical configuration, or operating process was completed.

02Observed Result

A result was directly visible in the system, operating environment, or available records.

03Client-Reported Outcome

The client reported an outcome. The case labels it instead of presenting it as independently measured.

04Prior Operating Experience

The work predates Raptor Consulting Group and is disclosed as professional experience.

Claim Boundary // What the Records Do Not Promise

Past work is context, not a guarantee.

The cases demonstrate operating experience, technical judgment, and delivered systems. They do not promise identical traffic, revenue, throughput, rankings, or cost outcomes for a different business.

Results depend on the starting system, market, resources, implementation authority, measurement quality, and conditions outside Raptor's control.
